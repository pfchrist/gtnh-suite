🏆 Tier-by-Tier Machine Priority: What to Build First to Make Circuits Cheaper
ROI Roadmap

As you advance through GregTech voltage tiers, specific machines drastically slash your circuit manufacturing costs and craft times. Focus on building these high-ROI machines first:

LV TIER Low Voltage: Escaping the Manual Crafting Bottleneck
Top Circuit ROI
1. Circuit Assembler (LV) PRIORITY #1
Eliminates 100% of Steel Casings (saves 0.5 steel plate per circuit) and insulated rubber cables. Unlocks 4x Vacuum Tube yield with molten Red Alloy.
2. Wiremill (LV) PRIORITY #2
Doubles wire output: 1 Ingot → 2 Wires (vs 1 Ingot → 1 Wire with manual wire cutters). Cuts copper wire consumption by exactly 50%.
3. Chemical Bath (LV) PRIORITY #3
Automates wood plate coating with liquid Glue or Polyethylene to mass-produce Basic Circuit Boards without manual crafting tables.
MV TIER Medium Voltage: Silicon Boules & Chemical Etching
Massive Scaling
1. EBF + Cutter (Silicon Boule) PRIORITY #1
Melt 32 Silicon SG Dust + 1 Tiny GaAs Crystal (1784 K) → 16 Wafers → 128 ILC Chips + 512 RAM Chips. Permanently eliminates vacuum tubes!
2. Chemical Reactor (Iron III Chloride) PRIORITY #2
1 Iron Dust + 6 HCl → 2000 mB FeCl3. Requires 50% the fluid volume of Sodium Persulfate to etch Phenolic and Plastic boards!
3. Laser Engraver (MV) PRIORITY #3
Engraves Red Lens (ILC chips) and Cyan Lens (RAM chips). Unlocks Integrated Logic Circuits (ILC) and Good Integrated Circuits (GIC).
HV TIER High Voltage: Cleanroom, SMT Line & Plastic Substrates
16x-64x SMD Boost
1. Cleanroom + Laser (White Lens) PRIORITY #1
Unlocks CPU Wafers → Microprocessors (2x yield per craft) and Integrated Processors on Plastic Circuit Boards.
2. HV Assembler (Circuit 3 - SMD) PRIORITY #2
Mass-produces SMD Resistors (16x-32x), SMD Diodes (64x), SMD Capacitors (24x), and SMD Transistors (32x). Advanced Circuits craft in 20s instead of 40s!
3. PVC / PTFE Chemical Reactor PRIORITY #3
Synthesize PVC (2x Plastic Boards) or PTFE (4x Plastic Boards) per plate, saving 50% to 75% of copper foil and sulfuric acid.

1. Soldering Fluid Multipliers (SubstituteFluidStack) Fluid Math

When a Circuit Assembler recipe specifies a soldering fluid requirement (base 72 mB = 0.5 ingot), the game applies the material quality multiplier:

Soldering Alloy (1.0x Base) 72 mB per LV circuit • 144 mB per EV circuit. Lowest fluid volume, highly recommended.
Molten Tin (2.0x Multiplier) 144 mB per LV circuit • Consumes exactly 2x the raw metal ingots compared to Soldering Alloy.
Molten Lead (4.0x Multiplier) 288 mB per LV circuit • Consumes 4x the raw metal ingots. Emergency fallback only.
2. Silicon Boule Slicing & Laser Lens Chip Yields Silicon Line

A single Silicon Boule (32 Silicon SG Dust + 1 Tiny GaAs Crystal in EBF @ 1784 K) yields 16 Silicon Wafers in a Cutting Machine:

Cyan Lens (RAM Chips) 1 Wafer → 32 RAM Chips.
1 Boule = 512 RAM Chips! (Used in Advanced Circuits & Workstations).
White Lens (CPU Chips) 1 Wafer → 8 CPU Chips.
1 Boule = 128 CPU Chips! (Requires Cleanroom; for Microprocessors).
