⚗️ Chemical Etchant Efficiency: Iron(III) Chloride vs. Sodium Persulfate

When etching circuit boards in the Chemical Reactor or Chemical Bath, you must supply an etching solution to etch the copper/gold/electrum traces:

Iron(III) Chloride (FeCl3) - 2x Efficiency Optimal Etchant
  • ✅ Half the Volume: Requires exactly 50% of the fluid volume compared to Sodium Persulfate!
  • ✅ Phenolic Board: 100 mB FeCl3 vs. 200 mB Persulfate.
  • ✅ Plastic Board: 250 mB FeCl3 vs. 500 mB Persulfate.
  • ✅ Epoxy Board: 500 mB FeCl3 vs. 1000 mB Persulfate.
  • ✅ Recipe: 1 Iron Dust + 6000 mB Hydrochloric Acid → 2000 mB FeCl3 + 3000 mB Hydrogen. Easy to loop with chlorine regeneration!
Sodium Persulfate (Na2S2O8) - Early Fallback Early Alternate
  • ❌ Double the Volume: Consumes 2x more fluid per board craft.
  • ❌ Complex Synthesis: Requires Sodium Dust + 2 Sulfur + 8 Oxygen in Chemical Reactor.
  • ❌ Useful Early: Does not require chlorine or hydrochloric acid loops if chlorine is unavailable in early LV.
  • ⚠️ Recommendation: Switch to Iron(III) Chloride immediately upon reaching MV to cut chemical fluid logistics in half.
