A comprehensive, zero-dependency web companion suite for **GregTech: New Horizons 1.7.10**.

1. **🔨 Tinkers' Tool & Weapon Architect (`gtnh_tinkers_architect.html`)**
   - Interactive assembly workbench for all tool archetypes (Pickaxes, Hammers, Mattocks, Cleavers, Rapiers, Crossbows, Shurikens, Shields).
   - 72 Curated Blueprints spanning Stone Age to LuV/Space progression.
   - Dual Tool Comparator with side-by-side spec deltas and win indicators.
   - Live Part-Swap Candidate Matrix tailored to archetype-specific stats.
   - 140+ GTNH 2.9 material stats, modifier planners, and game formula tooltips.

2. **⚙️ GregTech Tool, Maintenance & Prospecting Architect (`gtnh_gregtech_tools.html`)**
   - Curated high-ROI material recommendations across Steam, LV, MV, HV, and EV tiers.
   - Multiblock Maintenance diagnostic solver mapping all 6 error codes to exact repair tools.
   - Analog & Electric Prospector radar optimizer with interactive 3x3 vein grid & edge scan algorithms.

   - Deep GT5U game-code codex for butchery knife looting, in-world crushing & smeltery plunging.

3. **⚡ Circuit Evolution & Material Tradeoff Architect (`gtnh_circuit_architect.html`)**
   - Interactive Circuit BOM & Scaler with exact raw metal and chemical requirements from ULV Vacuum Tubes up to EV / AE2.

   - Chemical Etchant Efficiency: Iron(III) Chloride (50% volume) vs. Sodium Persulfate & acid coating yields.
   - Road to AE2 Pathway: Milestone tech ladder & starter AE2 infrastructure circuit budget estimator.

4. **⛏️ Dimensional Ore & Yield Navigator (`gtnh_ore_navigator.html`)**
   - 122 code-verified ore mix veins across Overworld, Nether, Twilight Forest & Space.
   - Multi-tier processing yield calculators (Stone ➔ Steam ➔ LV ➔ MV Chem Bath ➔ HV+).
   - "Which Vein Should I Mine?" reverse target ranking engine.
   - Small ore indicators, vein dimensions, and harvest level thresholds.

5. **🍲 Food & Nutrition Architect (`gtnh_food_architect.html`)**
   - 755 Cataloged Foods with hunger, saturation, and nutrition categories.

   - 8 Canonical Wild Gardens & 133 Base Pantry Sourcing Inspector.
   - Tech Gate Timeline (Stone Age ➔ Steam ➔ LV ➔ MV ➔ HV+).

6. **🐝 Bee Breeding & Genetic Navigator (`gtnh_bee_navigator.html`)**
   - Forward & reverse genetic mutation solver across Forestry, Extra Bees, Magic Bees, and GTNH.
   - Climate tolerances, diurnal traits, and comb centrifuge drop tables.

7. **🌾 IC2 Crop Mutation & Breeding Suite (`gtnh_cropsnh_navigator.html`)**
