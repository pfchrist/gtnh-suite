🗺️ F9 Chunk Center & Ore Vein Grid Calculator

In GT5u / GTNH 2.9, ore veins generate in 3 × 3 chunk sectors (48 × 48 blocks). The vein center chunk always satisfies: abs(ChunkX) % 3 == 1 and abs(ChunkZ) % 3 == 1.

🔍 1. Press F9 Three Times (Chunk Grid)

• Green Lines: Bounding box of the 3 × 3 chunk ore vein sector (48 × 48 blocks).
• Blue Lines: Mark the exact center chunk of the vein where the generation vertical shaft originates.
• Red Lines: Individual 16 × 16 chunk borders. Digging a 1 × 2 shaft at the center chunk coordinates (X: 8, Z: 8 relative to chunk) guarantees hitting the vein if one spawned.

💭 2. How Small Ore Indicators Work

• Small ores generate as single-block deposits per chunk in specific altitude bands independent of large veins.
• Indicativeness & Specificity: Spotting a small ore (e.g. exposed in ravines, cliffs, or cave ceilings) immediately limits the possible large motherlode veins in that chunk sector!
• Unique Indicators (🎯 100%): Small Lapis, Small Ruby, Small Diamond, Small Emerald, Small Ledox point exclusively to ONE specific vein!

🔦 3. JourneyMap & VisualProspecting

• Prospector's Wand: Shift-right click ground with a tool to reveal ores in a column.
• Electric Seismic Prospector: Unlocked at LV/MV, automatically maps a 5 × 5 vein radius (15 × 15 chunks) onto your fullscreen JourneyMap.
• Discovered veins are permanently recorded in your client save under visualprospecting/.
