Tinkers' Modifiers & Unbreakable Engineering

Recipes, upgrade thresholds, extra slot crafting, and Unbreakable X mechanics.

🛡️ Reaching 100% Unbreakable (Reinforced X)

Every level of Reinforced grants a flat 20% chance to not consume durability upon use. Stacking to Reinforced X (Level 10) grants 100% chance, making the tool completely Unbreakable forever!

Recipe: 1x Reinforced Plate = 1x Blank Cast + 8x Obsidian Blocks in Tool Forge.
Cost: 1 Modifier Slot per plate applied (+1 Reinforced level).

💡 Pro-Tip: Use naturally Reinforced materials (e.g. Damascus Steel, Knightmetal, Shadow Iron, Tartarite) to start with Reinforced II-IV, saving 2-4 modifier slots!

⭐ Extra Modifier Slot Recipes

You can craft extra modifier slots onto any tool in the Tool Forge:

Slot #1: 1x Diamond + 1x Block of Gold
Slot #2: 1x Block of Diamond + 1x Enchanted Golden Apple (Notch Apple)
Slot #3: 1x Nether Star
Slot #4: 1x Infinity Catalyst (+5 Modifier Slots!)

💡 Plus parts with Writable (Paper) or Thaumic (Thaumium) add +1 modifier slot per part!

⚡ Excavator 1-Tick Benchmark & Redstone Haste

1-Tick Digging Rule: At 22 Mining Speed, an Excavator digs dirt and sand in exactly 1 tick. Any extra Redstone beyond 22 speed has 0 benefit on standard dirt/sand! Divert all subsequent slots into Reinforced plates for Unbreakable status.

Tier 1 (50 Redstone): +0.08 speed/ea (+4.0 total speed)
Tier 2 (100 Redstone): +0.06 speed/ea (+7.0 total speed)
Tier 3 (150 Redstone): +0.04 speed/ea (+9.0 total speed)
Crossbow hard cap: 0.25 seconds minimum draw time!
💎 Lapis Lazuli (Fortune / Looting)

Applies Fortune to mining tools or Looting to weapons. Requires 1 initial modifier slot, then stacks:

Fortune I / Looting I: 100 Lapis Lazuli
Fortune II / Looting II: 300 Lapis Lazuli
Fortune III / Looting III: 450 Lapis Lazuli
💀 Beheading & Skullfire Strategy

Increases the drop rate of Mob Skulls and Wither Skeleton Skulls (critical in HV for NASA Workbench).

Recipe: 1x Ender Pearl + 1x Obsidian Block per level.
Each level adds +10% beheading probability.
Cleavers come with inherent Beheading II (+20%)!
🌱 Ball of Moss (Auto-Repair)

Passive durability regeneration in sunlight/daylight.

Recipe: 9x Mossy Cobblestone / Mossy Stone Bricks in Crafting Table.
Cost: 1 Modifier Slot per Ball of Moss. Stacks repair speed. 2x Moss recommended for Crossbow Bolts & Shurikens!
